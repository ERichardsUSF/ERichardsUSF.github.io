About Sideline

Meet the developer and learn how this sports-team website was created.

Our concept

Sideline — Find Your Team is a sports-team directory that helps visitors discover teams across soccer, basketball, and baseball. Visitors can search and filter teams, save favorites, manage sample team records, and explore sports data through charts.

Meet the developer

I am a graduate student in AI and Business Analytics at the University of South Florida. I work in HR operations and have experience with business processes, data analysis, and technology. Building Sideline has helped me understand how user-friendly websites are structured and how interactive features improve the user experience.

How I built it

I created Sideline using HTML for the structure, CSS for its visual design and responsive layout, and JavaScript for searching, filtering, favorites, and managing team records. The Analytics page uses Chart.js to present team data visually. Team changes are stored locally in the browser for this classroom demonstration, rather than in a database. The website is deployed using GitHub Pages.
